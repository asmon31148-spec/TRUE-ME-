Privacy Policy for True Me

Effective Date: November 25, 2026 · Android Package: com.trueme.study.trueme

True Me ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting student privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what data True Me collects, how it is processed, and how you control your files and accounts.

1. Data You Provide & Local-First Storage

  • Profile & Study Preferences: Your display name, email address, subjects, learning style, and exam target dates are stored locally on your device and synced to your authenticated Supabase workspace.
  • Study Materials & Progress: Extracted text from your study notes, SM-2 flashcard schedules, quiz scores, study tasks, and recent sessions are cached locally using Hive and synced for cross-device continuity.

2. Google Drive Integration & Limited Use Disclosure

Google API Services User Data Policy Compliance: True Me's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • Requested Scope: True Me strictly requests the non-sensitive https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file scope only.
  • What We Access: True Me only reads, creates, or updates files that you explicitly open inside True Me or export from True Me into your personal True Me folder on Google Drive. True Me never accesses or scans other files in your Google Drive.
  • No Ads or Resale: Google Drive user data is never sold, rented, shared with advertisers, or used to train public AI models.

3. AI Processing

When you ask Sage a question or request a quiz, flashcard deck, summary, mind map, or study guide export, only the relevant prompt and selected subject text are sent to configured AI providers strictly to generate your requested output.

4. Data Retention, Disconnect & Deletion

  • Disconnect Google Drive: Open Account → Storage → Google Drive → Disconnect to immediately revoke OAuth access tokens while keeping your local notes intact.
  • Delete Local Data: Use Account → Storage → Clear Local Cache or Account → Data → Remove from App.
  • Full Account Deletion: Use Account → Data → Delete Everything to purge local storage, Supabase database rows, and the True Me folder inside your Google Drive.

5. Contact & Support

For privacy inquiries, Google OAuth feedback, or data deletion requests, contact the True Me developer at asmon31148@gmail.com or via the True Me GitHub repository.
