TRUE ME — User Data Deletion Instructions

TRUE ME respects your privacy and gives you full control over your account and study data.

1. Delete Your Data Directly Inside the App

  1. Open the TRUE ME Android app.
  2. Go to Account (top-right profile icon).
  3. Scroll to Data and tap Delete Everything.
  4. Type DELETE to permanently erase your cloud profile, notebooks, study tasks, flashcards, quiz history, and connected cloud vault folder.

2. Request Deletion by Email

If you no longer have access to the app, send a deletion request from your registered email address to asif123king31148@gmail.com with the subject TRUE ME Data Deletion Request. Your account and associated records will be permanently removed.
