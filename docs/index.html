How True Me Uses Google Drive

True Me respects your privacy and keeps your heavy study files under your own control:

  • Minimal Scope Only (drive.file): True Me only accesses the specific study files you import into the app or export into your personal True Me folder. It cannot see or modify unrelated files in your Google Drive.
  • Your Storage, Your Ownership: Uploaded PDFs, lecture images, and generated PDF/DOCX/PPTX study guides are saved inside your own Google Drive True Me folder so you retain full ownership and storage control.
  • One-Tap Revoke: Disconnect Google Drive or remove stored app data anytime inside Account → Storage / Data.
